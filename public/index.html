About Muskura Group

An early-stage group with a long-term view.

MUSKURA GROUP is at the beginning of its journey. We are building useful, scalable businesses that serve people in Africa and can grow to serve people around the world.

We start small, learn from real users and keep improving. We are open about where each venture stands today.

Our principleAdapt, Change and Move Fast.

Mission

To build self-sustaining, useful ventures that solve everyday challenges in technology, information and employment across African markets.

Vision

To grow into a trusted group of businesses, recognised across Africa and beyond for useful technology, honest media and real opportunity.

Values

Integrity. Practical usefulness. Speed with care. Responsible growth.

Our approach

  1. 1

    Find real problems

    Start with what people actually struggle with.

  2. 2

    Build practical solutions

    Make something simple that works first.

  3. 3

  5. 5

Founder's note

Why Muskura Group exists

Africa has no shortage of ideas, talent or problems worth solving. What it needs is more people willing to build practical solutions and stay with them.

We are at the start of that work. We do not have everything figured out, and we will not pretend to. What we do have is a commitment to move fast, listen carefully and build things that are genuinely useful.

If that sounds like something you want to be part of, as a user, a partner or a collaborator, we would like to hear from you.

Musa Salihu KuruFounder & CEO, MUSKURA GROUP
