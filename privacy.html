1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MUSKURA GROUP ("MUSKURA GROUP", "we", "us") handles personal information when you visit this website or contact us through it.

MUSKURA GROUP is an early-stage venture group based in Abuja FCT, Nigeria. This policy applies to this website only. It does not cover our pages on social media platforms or any other website we link to. Those are run by other companies and have their own privacy policies.

We have tried to keep this policy short and plain. If anything is unclear, email us at MUSKURAGROUP@GMAIL.COM.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. If you use the contact form, we receive:

  • your name and email address;
  • the option you choose under "I am a" (for example, potential user, investor or business partner);
  • the message you write, and anything else you choose to include in it.

If you email us directly, we receive your email address, your name if it shows in your email, and your message. Please do not include sensitive information, such as health details, ID numbers or bank details, in a message to us.

Information collected automatically. This website does not use analytics tools, advertising trackers or user accounts. However, when your browser loads the website, technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device details, the pages requested and the time of the request is normally received by the companies that deliver the site and its resources. These include the service that hosts the website and Google Fonts (see section 4). Those companies may keep logs for security and operational reasons.

Spam protection. The contact form contains a hidden field that real visitors never see or fill in. It helps us spot automated spam and does not collect personal information.

3. How we use information

We use the information you give us to:

  • respond to your enquiry;
  • communicate with you about what you contacted us about, such as a venture, partnership or collaboration;
  • maintain and protect the website;
  • prevent spam, abuse and unauthorised access;
  • meet legal obligations that apply to us.

We do not sell personal information collected through this website. We will not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Where Nigerian data protection law requires a lawful basis, we rely on your consent when you choose to send us a message, on our legitimate interests in answering enquiries and keeping the website safe, and on legal obligations where they apply.

4. Contact form and third-party services

The contact form is handled by Formspree (formspree.io), an online form service. When you press "Send message", your name, email address, chosen role and message are sent from your browser to Formspree. Formspree processes the submission and passes it to our email inbox. Formspree is an independent company. It may also collect technical details, such as your IP address, while handling your submission, and it may store submissions on its own systems. We do not control how Formspree handles information. Please read the privacy policy published on Formspree's website to see how it does so.

We receive enquiries at a Gmail address, so the messages we receive are also handled by Google as our email provider, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

At the time of this update, the website's own code does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and does not store tracking information in your browser (for example, in local storage).

Companies that deliver parts of the website, such as the hosting service, Google Fonts and Formspree, have their own practices, which we do not control. If you follow a link to a social media platform or another website, that site may set its own cookies under its own policies.

If we add analytics or similar tools in future, we will update this policy.

6. Sharing information

We share personal information only where it is reasonably necessary, for example with:

  • service providers that help us run the website and handle enquiries (such as those in section 4);
  • professional advisers, where we need their advice;
  • authorities or other parties, where the law requires it or where we need to protect our rights, our users or the security of the website.

We cannot promise that information will never be shared, because the law may sometimes require us to disclose it.

7. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as is reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, for example to answer your enquiry and any follow-up, and for as long as the law requires. We have not set a fixed retention period. When we no longer need the information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

Our service providers may keep their own copies according to their own policies. You can ask us about information we hold, and you can read their policies for information they hold.

8. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information. For example, the contact form sends your submission to Formspree over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we aim to limit access to enquiries to the people who need them.

No website, internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee the security of information you send us. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

9. Your privacy rights

MUSKURA GROUP is based in Nigeria, so the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 applies to our handling of personal information. Under that law and other applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information in certain circumstances;
  • ask us to restrict or stop certain uses of your information;
  • receive your information in a commonly used format where the law allows;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, which does not affect what we did before you withdrew it.

These rights are subject to conditions and exceptions in the law.

How to use your rights. Email MUSKURAGROUP@GMAIL.COM with the subject "Privacy request". Tell us your name, the email address you used to contact us, and what you would like us to do. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We will respond within the time that applicable law requires.

Concerns and complaints. Please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, the regulator under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

If you live outside Nigeria, the law where you live may give you additional rights. We will consider requests under the laws that apply to us. We do not assume that every country's law applies to every visitor.

10. International visitors and data transfers

This website can be visited from anywhere in the world. Some of the services described in section 4, such as Formspree and Google, may process information on servers outside Nigeria. We have not independently verified where each provider stores information. By sending us a message, you understand that your information may be processed in other countries by these providers. We choose established providers and rely on the safeguards they publish, and we do not guarantee those safeguards ourselves.

11. Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults and organisations. It is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Nigerian law has special rules about children's information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please email us and we will delete it where appropriate.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new services to the website. The updated version will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date at the top will change. If a change is significant, we will say so on the website. Please check back from time to time.
